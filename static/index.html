How we calculate

Daily water need: max(0, ET0 × Kc − rain). ET0 and rain come from the Open-Meteo forecast; 1 mm over 1 ha = 10 m³.

Kc — mid-season value from FAO-56, Table 12. For the selected crop: —.

Diesel: area × (L/h ÷ ha/h) — one machine pass. CO₂: litres × 2.65 kg/L (IPCC 2006).

Soil, growth stage and irrigation losses are not modelled — this is an estimate.
